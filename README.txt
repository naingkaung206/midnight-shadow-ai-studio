Midnight Shadow AI Studio — Free Starter Prototype

How to use:
1. Open index.html in a modern browser (Chrome recommended).
2. Fill in story title, category, duration, setting, and idea.
3. Tap “ဇာတ်ညွှန်းနှင့် Prompt ဖန်တီးမယ်”.
4. Switch tabs to view the script, scene prompts, voice/caption plan, and posting checklist.
5. Copy text or export the current tab as a TXT file.

Important limitations:
- This is a front-end prototype. It does not call a real AI model, render video, generate audio, or publish to social platforms yet.
- True AI generation needs a server/backend and an AI provider API. API keys must never be placed in browser HTML.
- Auto-posting requires approved official platform APIs/OAuth and may be limited by platform policy and account eligibility.
- Hosting may be free on some providers, but free tiers and limits can change.
